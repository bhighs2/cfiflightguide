BeforeTakeoff Check(Runup)
Objective
ToensuretheapplicantlearnsthepurposeofandcanexhibitaclearunderstandingoftheBeforeTakeoffCheckprocedureandhowtoperformtheprocedureproperly.
Purpose
Nopilotwantstobeconfrontedwithanunexpectedenginefailureorfailureoftheflightcontrolsatanypointduringaflight,buttheseareespeciallydangerousontakeoff. TheBeforeTakeoffCheckandRunupareimportantprocedureswhichwillensurethattheengineandairplaneareairworthyandfunctioningproperly. ThislessonintroducestheBeforeTakeoffCheckandRunupproceduresandtheimportanceofaPre-DepartureBriefing.
